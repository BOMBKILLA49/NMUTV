Socorro's dedicated UTV repair shop

NM UTV is a full-service UTV and side-by-side repair shop in Socorro, NM. We fix sport machines, hunting rigs, ranch side-by-sides and oil field work UTVs, and we serve riders across all of New Mexico with pickup and delivery.

We do everything from installing new radios to engine work, engine swaps, suspension work, new tires and wheels, belt installations and crash repairs. If it is a side-by-side, we can fix it.

Owned by a factory Can-Am racer

NM UTV proudly serves the entire state of New Mexico, owned by a former member of the factory Can-Am ATV racing team, Dillon Zimmerman (see his WORCS ATV Pro results), with a long history in the powersports industry. That racing background shows up in how we work: we know how these machines are built, how they fail, and how to make them last. Our mechanics are certified and have been working on side-by-sides for years, our turnaround times are quick, and our prices are very competitive. Over the years, we’ve seen how dealerships and other shops take advantage of customers, and we’re here to change that. We’re bringing New Mexico a UTV repair source that delivers a level of honesty, quality, and expertise the state has never experienced before.

Where is NM UTV located?
