Desert Trail Ride

A quick ride on the wide open BLM desert trails just outside Socorro. Great for first-timers and anyone passing through on I-25.

Request this tour Call 575-418-0601
  • Private, your group only
  • Up to 2 riders per UTV
  • Experienced local guides
  • Helmets & goggles included

Best for: First-timers, families and anyone passing through Socorro on I-25 who wants a quick taste of the desert.

Where we go

  1. Check in at the shop

    Meet us at 1261 Frontage Rd NW in Socorro. Sign waivers and get fitted for helmets and goggles.

  2. Safety briefing and riding lesson

    Your guide goes over the controls, hand signals and how to handle sand and washes. No experience needed.

  3. Ride the BLM desert

    Head out onto the wide open Bureau of Land Management desert just outside town. Two-track trails, sandy washes and big views of Socorro Peak and the surrounding mountains.

  4. Photo stop

    Stop at a scenic overlook for pictures before riding back.

  5. Back to the shop

    Return to the shop about 1.5 to 2 hours after you leave.

What's included

  • Guide and 2-seat UTV
  • Helmets and goggles
  • Safety briefing and riding lesson
  • Water

What to bring

  • Closed-toe shoes and long pants
  • Layers, plus a jacket and gloves in winter
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Phone or camera for pictures
  • Driver's license if you're driving

Good to know

  • Morning, afternoon and sunset times available
  • Lunch is not included
  • The route changes with trail conditions and your riding level
  • Every rider signs a waiver. Drivers need a valid driver's license.
  • If we cancel for weather, you can reschedule or get a full refund.
