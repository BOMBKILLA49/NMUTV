Half Day Explorer

More trail time, more hidden landmarks and more ground covered across the BLM desert around Socorro.

Request this tour Call 575-418-0601
  • Private, your group only
  • Up to 2 riders per UTV
  • Experienced local guides
  • Helmets & goggles included

Best for: Riders who want more trail time and more ground covered without committing a whole day.

Where we go

  1. Check in at the shop

    Meet us at 1261 Frontage Rd NW in Socorro. Sign waivers and get fitted for helmets and goggles.

  2. Safety briefing

    Your guide covers the controls and the plan for the day.

  3. Desert trails

    Ride farther out into the BLM desert than the shorter tours go, with washes, ridgelines and wide open views.

  4. Hidden landmarks

    Visit hidden spots around the Socorro Valley that most locals don't know about, like old mines and historic landmarks.

  5. Snack break

    Take a break in the shade with water and snacks.

  6. Back to the shop

    Return to the shop about 4 hours after you leave.

What's included

  • Guide and 2-seat UTV
  • Helmets and goggles
  • Hidden landmark stops
  • Water and snacks

What to bring

  • Closed-toe shoes and long pants
  • Layers, plus a jacket and gloves in winter
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Phone or camera for pictures
  • Driver's license if you're driving

Good to know

  • Morning or afternoon start
  • Lunch is not included, but snacks and water are
  • The route changes with trail conditions and your riding level
  • Every rider signs a waiver. Drivers need a valid driver's license.
  • If we cancel for weather, you can reschedule or get a full refund.
