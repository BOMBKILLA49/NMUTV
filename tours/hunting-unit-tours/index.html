Hunting unit tour FAQ

Which units do you cover?

We cover hunting units across New Mexico. Select your unit when you request a tour and we'll confirm the details. Units far from Socorro may add a travel fee, which we quote before you book.

When should I book?

Book as soon as you draw your tag. Most hunters tour their unit in the weeks before opening day.

What do you show me?

Your unit boundaries, roads and access points, where public land meets private land, and the do's and don'ts for hunting that unit.

How is pricing per UTV?

Each price covers one 2-seat UTV and a guide for up to 2 hunters, including meals for 2. Hunting with a bigger group? Book more UTVs.

Do I need my own camping gear for the weekend tour?

We set up the tent camp for you. Bring your sleeping bag and personal gear, and we'll send you a full packing list when you book.
