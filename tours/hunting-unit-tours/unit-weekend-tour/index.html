Weekend Unit Tour + Camp

Two days touring your unit with a tent camp set up for you inside the unit, plus every meal. You learn the country and stay right where you'll hunt.

Request this tour Call 575-418-0601
  • Private, your group only
  • Up to 2 hunters per UTV
  • Experienced local guides
  • Helmets & goggles included

Best for: Hunters who want to learn their unit and stay right in it, with camp and meals taken care of.

Where we go

  1. Day 1: Meet up and breakfast

    Meet your guide and start with breakfast.

  2. Day 1: Tour your unit

    Ride your unit boundaries, roads and access points, and see where public and private land meet.

  3. Day 1: Camp setup

    We set up a tent camp for you inside your unit.

  4. Day 1: Dinner at camp

    Dinner at camp, included for 2.

  5. Day 2: Breakfast at camp

    Wake up in your unit with breakfast at camp.

  6. Day 2: More of your unit

    Cover the parts of the unit you didn't see on day 1 and go over the do's and don'ts for your hunt.

  7. Day 2: Lunch and dinner

    Lunch in the field and dinner, included for 2.

What's included

  • Guide and 2-seat UTV both days
  • Tent camp set up in your unit
  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner each day for 2
  • Unit boundaries and access
  • Do's and don'ts for your unit

What to bring

  • Closed-toe shoes and long pants
  • Layers, plus a jacket and gloves in winter
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Phone or camera for pictures
  • Driver's license if you're driving
  • Your hunting tag info, maps and GPS

Good to know

  • Bring your sleeping bag and personal gear. We send a full packing list when you book.
  • Units far from Socorro may add a travel fee, quoted before you book
  • Book as soon as you draw your tag
  • Every rider signs a waiver. Drivers need a valid driver's license.
  • If we cancel for weather, you can reschedule or get a full refund.
