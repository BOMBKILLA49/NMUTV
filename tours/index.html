Where we ride

BLM desert trails

Miles of wide open Bureau of Land Management desert surround Socorro. Washes, ridgelines, mesas and views of the Magdalena and San Mateo mountains. Your guide picks the trails based on your riding experience.

Hidden landmarks

The Socorro Valley is full of places most locals don't know about: a lone mine in the middle of nowhere with a door frame still standing on the mountainside, historic landmarks, and arroyos and trails you can't reach by car or truck. We take you to them and tell you the stories behind them.

Lunch in San Antonio

San Antonio, New Mexico is a tiny town with a big reputation for green chile cheeseburgers. Full day tours stop for lunch at the Buckhorn Tavern or the Owl Bar & Cafe.

Bosque del Apache

The Bosque del Apache National Wildlife Refuge is just south of San Antonio along the Rio Grande. In the winter it fills with sandhill cranes, snow geese and other birds, and it's one of the best wildlife viewing spots in the Southwest.

UTV tour FAQ

How is pricing per UTV?

Each price covers one 2-seat UTV and a guide, for up to 2 riders. Come alone or bring a passenger, the price is the same. Need more seats? Book more UTVs.

Are tours private?

Yes. Every tour is just your group and your guide. We never put strangers together.

When do tours run?

Short tours run in morning, afternoon and sunset time slots. The full day tour starts in the morning. We have a limited number of UTVs and guides, so book early, especially on weekends.

Who can drive?

Drivers need a valid driver's license. Passengers of all ages are welcome as long as they can wear a helmet and sit safely in the seat. Every rider signs a waiver.

What should I wear?

Closed-toe shoes, long pants and layers. It gets dusty, so bring sunglasses or use the goggles we provide. Bring sunscreen, and a jacket in the winter.

What if the weather is bad?

If we cancel for weather, you can reschedule or get a full refund.

Where do tours start?

Tours meet at our shop, 1261 Frontage Rd NW, Socorro, NM 87801, right off I-25.

Do you offer tours for hunters?

Yes. See our hunting unit tours, where we show you your unit before your hunt.
