Socorro to San Antonio Full Day

Our signature tour. BLM desert trails, hidden landmarks, lunch in San Antonio at the Buckhorn or the Owl Bar, and the Bosque del Apache National Wildlife Refuge.

Request this tour Call 575-418-0601
  • Private, your group only
  • Up to 2 riders per UTV
  • Experienced local guides
  • Helmets & goggles included

Best for: Anyone who wants the full Socorro County experience: desert riding, history, great food and wildlife in one day.

Where we go

  1. Morning check-in

    Meet us at the shop at 1261 Frontage Rd NW in Socorro. Sign waivers, get fitted for helmets and goggles, and get a safety briefing.

  2. BLM desert trails

    Spend the morning riding the wide open BLM desert around Socorro.

  3. Hidden landmarks

    Stop at hidden spots around the Socorro Valley that most people don't know are out there.

  4. Lunch in San Antonio

    Head south to the tiny town of San Antonio, New Mexico for lunch at the Buckhorn Tavern or the Owl Bar & Cafe, both known for their green chile cheeseburgers. Lunch for 2 is included.

  5. Bosque del Apache

    Explore the Bosque del Apache National Wildlife Refuge along the Rio Grande just south of San Antonio. In the winter it fills with sandhill cranes and snow geese.

  6. Back to Socorro

    Return to the shop in the late afternoon, 6 to 8 hours after you leave.

What's included

  • Guide and 2-seat UTV
  • Helmets and goggles
  • Lunch for 2 in San Antonio
  • Bosque del Apache visit
  • Hidden landmark stops
  • Water and snacks

What to bring

  • Closed-toe shoes and long pants
  • Layers, plus a jacket and gloves in winter
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Phone or camera for pictures
  • Driver's license if you're driving

Good to know

  • Starts in the morning
  • Lunch for 2 in San Antonio is included
  • Crane season at the Bosque is usually November through February
  • The route changes with trail conditions and restaurant hours
  • Every rider signs a waiver. Drivers need a valid driver's license.
  • If we cancel for weather, you can reschedule or get a full refund.
