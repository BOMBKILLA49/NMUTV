Clayton UTV shop & side-by-side repair

NM UTV handles Clayton side-by-side repair for sport riders, hunters, ranchers and work crews. Our shop is in Socorro, but we pick up and deliver UTVs from Clayton and the rest of Union County, so a trip to the shop is as easy as a phone call.

Around Clayton and eastern New Mexico, riding usually means farm and ranch ground, caliche roads and sand. On the eastern plains, side-by-sides are mostly working machines on farms, dairies and ranches. Routine maintenance, brakes, wheel bearings, CV axles, belts and electrical repairs keep them on the job, and the sand hills are tough on clutches and belts.

Clayton Polaris repair: RZR 1000, RZR 900, Ranger & more

Need a Polaris shop for Clayton? We service and repair every Polaris side-by-side, including the RZR XP 1000, RZR Pro XP, Turbo R, RZR 900 and Trail 900, Rangers and Generals. Common jobs for Clayton Polaris owners include belts, clutches, radius rods, ball joints, wheel bearings and full engine rebuilds.

Clayton Can-Am repair: Maverick X3, Defender & Commander

Our owner raced on the factory Can-Am team, so Can-Am machines are close to home for us. For Clayton Can-Am X3 repair we handle belts, clutches, turbo and boost problems, trailing arms, bearing carriers, engine rebuilds and crash damage, along with Maverick R, Maverick Sport, Maverick Trail, Commander and Defender models.

Honda, Yamaha, Kawasaki & CFMOTO service

Clayton UTV repair FAQ

Where is NM UTV located, and how far is it from Clayton?

NM UTV is located at 1261 Frontage Rd NW, Socorro, NM 87801, roughly 320 miles from Clayton. We serve Clayton and Union County with pickup and delivery for a fee, so you don't need a trailer to get your UTV to our shop.

Do you offer UTV pickup and delivery in Clayton?

Yes. We pick up side-by-sides in Clayton and anywhere else in New Mexico, repair them at our Socorro shop, and deliver them back when the work is done. Pickup and delivery is available for a fee based on distance. Call 575-418-0601 for a quote.

Can you repair my Polaris RZR 1000 or Can-Am X3 from Clayton?

Absolutely. Polaris RZR XP 1000, RZR 900, Pro XP and Ranger models and Can-Am Maverick X3, Maverick R and Defender models are among the machines we work on most. We handle everything from belts and clutches to engine rebuilds and engine swaps.

What UTV repairs do you do for Clayton customers?

Everything from installing new radios and stereos to engine work, engine swaps, suspension work, new tires and wheels, belt installations, clutch service, crash repairs, electrical diagnosis, lighting and accessory installs, and routine maintenance.

How long does a UTV repair take?

Our turnaround time is quick. Most maintenance, belt and accessory jobs are done fast, and we will give you a clear timeline after diagnosing bigger jobs like engine rebuilds or crash repairs, which can depend on parts availability.
